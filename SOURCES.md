## Method

- Primary anchors: the 24 November 2026 morning roundups of Jagran
  Josh, Studmentor, Observer Voice, The Daily Jagran, Education Times
  and Newsmriti, cross-checked against wire and outlet reporting
  (Reuters, AP, CNN, USA Today, NY Post, TBS News, BusinessWorld,
  Medianet, Aktualita, Europe Says, Access24, myKhel, CNET).
- Two sources per story where possible; stories appearing in only one
  of the checked sources are marked (single source) below.
- Wrong-day items found during research were DROPPED, not carried:
